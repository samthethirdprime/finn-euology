The Eulogy of Finn Michael Kariuki Ndegwa

Birth and Early Life

Finn Michael Kariuki Ndegwa, fondly referred to as Finn, was born at Ruai Family Hospital on the 15th of June 2018 at 1045hrs, to loving parents Dan Ndegwa Kariuki and Margaret Wangari Muriu. He was a doting brother to 10-month-old Quilan Mathew Muriu Ndegwa, and Half brother to Samara Mary Wangari. He was dedicated to the Lord on 26th March 2023, at Mamlaka Hill Chapel, City Campus where his family worships. He is survived by paternal grandparents Francis Kariuki Kimani and Esther Wanjiru Kariuki, and maternal grandparents Willy Muriu Mwaura and Elizabeth Njoki Muriu. Finn was a beloved nephew to Uncles Anthony Mwangi, Edward Kimani, Samuel Kamau, Alex Mwaura, Roland Ebole and Norbert Mburu. He will be remembered fondly by Aunties Ann Njeri, June Nduta, Lyne Nyakio, Vera Atieno, Janet Wangari and Vanessa Ndinda. Finn shared endless memories with Cousins Sandra, Gian, Jayden, Ellyn, Edison, Tasha, Imani, Kenna, Morani and Layi.

School and Character

He started his school journey in the year 2021 at Garvin's Academy at Fedha, Embakasi, before later moving to St. Vincent Pallotti Academy in Dagoretti. At school, he was a very sharp boy whose favourite subjects were Mathematics and English. He was well connected with his classmates and was even trusted to serve as a class prefect. He was deeply loved by most of the children for his generosity and good manners, and he never got into trouble with his teachers, standing out as one of the most disciplined pupils at school. Many of us remember Finn as kind, energetic, intelligent and attentive; traits that came to life at St. Vincent Pallotti, where he was a Grade Three student. He had many hobbies, including playing football, swimming, cycling, skating, memory games, video games and going on adventurous trips with his family. He had a vast collection of toy cars, whose brand names he knew by heart, thanks to his sharp memory.

Ailment and Hospitalization

Finn accidentally hurt his left knee in March 2026, while playing with friends at their home in Nairobi. He was promptly taken to hospital, where upon a scan and review, he was given medication and discharged. After about three weeks, his condition did not improve, and out of concern, his parents took him back to hospital for further check up. On this visit, he was diagnosed with a lung infection and pneumonia, for which he received treatment. Unfortunately, despite medication, Finn’s health continued to deteriorate, and further tests showed symptoms associated with Tuberculosis. He was put on Tuberculosis medication in April 2026. During this time, Finn lost a lot of weight, and struggled with breathing and appetite. His health improved slightly but then took a turn for worse towards the end of April. Concerned, his father took him back to hospital on 7th May 2026 for a check up, as they were getting a refill of the Tuberculosis prescription. Upon this visit to Riruta Sub-county Hospital, the doctors were alarmed at the state of his breathing, among other test results, and immediately put him on oxygen. They further referred him to Mbagathi County Hospital because at the time all signs were showing that he was suffering from TB, (which is only treated at government facilities) where he was rushed and admitted on the same day. He remained at Mbagathi Hospital under the care of doctors and loved ones. Further tests showed a widespread infection in his lungs, which had also spread to his bones and spinal cord. On the back of this diagnosis he was referred to Kenyatta Hospital on 16th May 2026, for further treatment. At Kenyatta Prime Care Centre, he went through a wide range of tests and scans as the doctors and specialists worked to identify the root cause of his ailment. Finn would spend another three weeks at Kenyatta Hospital on oxygen and with marginal improvements to his health. Throughout the ordeal, Finn showed an incredible strength and grace that could only come from God. He smiled, he engaged in conversation, he diligently took his medication and submitted himself to the numerous tests without complaint.

Demise

On the night of Wednesday 10th June 2026, with Cucu Mama Tony by his side, Finn complained of a tummy ache. Despite efforts by the hospital staff to give him reprieve, Finn deteriorated rapidly and breathed his last at just a few minutes before 12 midnight. He was in grade 3 at the time of his demise. We will miss you deeply Finn, but knowing you are with Jesus, and relieved of all pain, gives us comfort. We thank the almighty God for the incredible 7 years and the awesome memories we created. Until we meet again, Rest in Peace champ.

Shule na Tabia

Alianza safari yake ya shule mwaka wa 2021 katika Chuo cha Garvin huko Fedha, Embakasi, kabla ya kuhamia Chuo cha St. Vincent Pallotti huko Dagoretti. Huko shuleni, alikuwa mvulana shupavu sana. Masomo aliopenda zaidi yalikuwa Hesabu na Kiingereza. Alisikizana sana na wanafunzi wenzake na hata aliaminika kutumikia kama kinara wa darasa. Alipendwa sana na watoto wengi kwa ukarimu wake na tabia njema, na hakuwahi kuingia katika matatizo na walimu wake, akionekana kuwa mmoja wa wanafunzi wenye nidhamu shuleni. Wengi wetu tunamkumbuka Finn kama mkarimu, mwenye nguvu, mwenye akili na makini; sifa ambazo zilikuja kuwa hai huko St. Vincent Pallotti, ambapo alikuwa mwanafunzi wa Darasa la Tatu. Alikuwa na mambo mengi ya kujifurahisha, kama kucheza mpira wa miguu, kuogelea, kuendesha baiskeli, kuteleza kwenye theluji, michezo ya kumbukumbu, michezo ya video na kwenda safari na familia yake. Alikuwa na mkusanyiko mkubwa wa magari ya kuchezea, ambayo majina ya chapa alijua kwa moyo, shukrani kwa kumbukumbu yake kali.

Ugonjwa na kulazwa hospitalini

Finn aliumia goti lake la kushoto kwa bahati mbaya mnamo Machi 2026, alipokuwa akicheza na marafiki nyumbani kwao Nairobi. Mara moja alipelekwa hospitalini, ambapo baada ya uchunguzi, alipewa dawa na kuruhusiwa kurudi Nyumbani. Baada ya wiki tatu hivi, hali yake haikutengemaa, na kwa wasiwasi, wazazi wake walimrudisha hospitalini kwa uchunguzi zaidi. Katika ziara hii, aligunduliwa na maambukizi ya mapafu na nimonia, ambayo alipata matibabu. Kwa bahati mbaya, licha ya dawa, afya ya Finn iliendelea kuzorota, na vipimo zaidi vilionyesha dalili zinazohusiana na Kifua kikuu. Aliwekwa kwenye dawa ya Kifua Kikuu mnamo Aprili 2026. Wakati huu, Finn alipoteza uzito mwingi, na alipata shida ya kupumua na hamu ya kula. Afya yake iliimarika kidogo lakini ikabadilika kuwa mbaya kuelekea mwisho wa Aprili. Akiwa na wasiwasi, babake alimrejesha hospitalini tarehe 7 Mei 2026 kwa uchunguzi, walipokuwa wakipokea dawa ya Kifua Kikuu. Katika ziara hii katika Hospitali ya Kaunti Ndogo ya Riruta, madaktari walishtushwa na hali yake ya kupumua, miongoni mwa matokeo mengine ya vipimo, na mara moja wakamwekea oksijeni. Aidha walimpeleka katika Hospitali ya Kaunti ya Mbagathi kwa sababu wakati huo dalili zote zilionyesha kuwa anaugua TB, (ambayo inatibiwa tu katika vituo vya serikali) ambapo alikimbizwa na kulazwa siku hiyo hiyo. Alisalia katika hospitali ya Mbagathi chini ya uangalizi wa madaktari na wapendwa wake. Uchunguzi zaidi ulionyesha maambukizi yaliyoenea katika mapafu yake, ambayo pia yalikuwa yameenea kwenye mifupa na uti wa mgongo. Kutokana na utambuzi huu alipewa rufaa ya kwenda Hospitali ya Kenyatta tarehe 16 Mei 2026, kwa matibabu zaidi. Katika kituo cha Kenyatta Prime Care Centre, alipitia vipimo na skani mbalimbali huku madaktari na wataalamu wakifanya kazi kubaini chanzo cha maradhi yake. Finn angekaa wiki nyingine tatu katika Hospitali ya Kenyatta kwa oksijeni na uboreshaji mdogo wa afya yake. Wakati wote wa jaribu hilo, Finn alionyesha nguvu na neema ya ajabu ambayo inaweza tu kutoka kwa Mungu. Alitabasamu, akajishughulisha na mazungumzo, alichukua dawa zake kwa bidii na kujisalimisha kwa vipimo vingi bila malalamiko.

Kifo Chake

Usiku wa Jumatano tarehe 10 Juni 2026, huku Cucu Mama Tony akiwa naye, Finn alilalamika kuhusu maumivu ya tumbo. Licha ya jitihada za wafanyakazi wa hospitali hiyo kumpa nafuu, Finn alidhoofika haraka na akakata roho dakika chache kabla ya saa 12 usiku wa manane. Alikuwa darasa la 3 wakati wa kifo chake. Tutakukumbuka sana Finn, lakini kujua uko pamoja na Yesu, na kuondolewa maumivu yote, hutufariji. Tunamshukuru Mungu Mwenyezi kwa miaka 7 ya ajabu na kumbukumbu nzuri tulizounda. Mpaka tukutane tena, Pumzika kwa Amani Champ.

Tribute by Uncle Norbert

I recall the excitement in the family back in 2018, when Finn was born. He was the first boy of the next generation in our family, and that made him special. And in many ways he was special; he had such a uniquely pure heart; so kind, simple, generous and lively. He was a genius with cars! I bet you he can name more car brands than all of us! At his core, Finn just wanted to play with his friends and go on adventures. That simplicity made him so easy to love. It was a great delight to see him grow into a beautiful boy. He seemed poised to be a tall guy (maybe even taller than me eventually.) It's so surreal writing this; this story was not meant to be like this. We dreamed that he would grow into a handsome young man, a solid first born to his siblings, a great car enthusiast and adventurer and a leader in his generation. But as Proverbs reminds us, “many are the plans in a man’s heart, but God’s purpose will prevail.” God purposed that he be with us for those 7 years, and God has purposed that he leaves us at this time. May He grant us the strength to find peace with this in time. Farewell our beautiful boy.
